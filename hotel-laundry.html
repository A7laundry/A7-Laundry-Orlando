Frequently asked questions

How do I leave my laundry for pickup?

Leave your bag with the hotel Front Desk or Bell Desk. We coordinate pickup and return with your accommodation whenever available.

What is the minimum order?

There is a $60 minimum per pickup. Pickup and delivery are included.

How long does it take?

Standard service is returned within up to 24 hours. Express service is returned within up to 8 hours, subject to availability.

Is Express service always available?

Express availability depends on the current schedule and capacity. Select Express while booking or contact us to confirm availability.

Are clothes washed separately?

Yes. Your laundry is handled separately and professionally cared for.

Can I request hypoallergenic detergent?

Yes. Tide Free & Gentle is available upon request. Please add this request when booking.

Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept card, Zelle and cash.

Which areas do you serve?

We serve the Orlando area, including hotels, resorts, Airbnbs, vacation homes and residences.

Do I need to create an account?

No. You can book online without creating an account.
