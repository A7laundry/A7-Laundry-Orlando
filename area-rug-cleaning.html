Why loose rugs are different

A rug holds everything a floor doesn't.

Unlike wall-to-wall carpet, an area rug is loose — which is exactly why it can be lifted, collected and cleaned properly. Every day it soaks up foot traffic, spills, pet accidents, dust and allergens deep into the pile.

Surface vacuuming barely touches it. We pick the rug up, deep-clean it at the right temperature for its fiber, lift out stains and odors, and return it looking and smelling new — for homeowners and vacation rental hosts alike.

Do you clean area rugs in Orlando?
Yes. A7 Laundry cleans loose area rugs across Orlando — oriental, wool, synthetic, cotton flatweave and shag. These are rugs that lie on top of your floor (not wall-to-wall carpet), so we can collect them, deep-clean them and bring them back fresh. Message us on WhatsApp at (407) 670-8839.
Do you pick up and deliver rugs?
Yes — rug cleaning pickup and delivery is included. Because area rugs are loose, we collect them from your home, clean them at our facility and deliver them back fresh. Free pickup & delivery for residents and vacation rental hosts across Orlando.
Do you clean oriental and wool rugs?
Yes. We clean oriental, wool, silk-blend and other delicate rugs with fiber-appropriate care — the right method, temperature and products for the material, so colors and fibers are protected. Tell us the rug type on WhatsApp and we'll confirm the best approach.
Can you remove stains and pet odors?
Yes. Our deep clean targets stains, trapped dirt, pet odors and allergens embedded in the pile, leaving the rug refreshed and deodorized. Older or set-in stains are treated as best-effort; we'll tell you honestly what to expect when you send a photo.
