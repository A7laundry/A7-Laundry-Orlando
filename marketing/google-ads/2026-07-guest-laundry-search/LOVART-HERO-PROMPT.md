# Lovart — Hero Guest Laundry Orlando

## Objetivo

Criar a fotografia principal da landing page da A7 Laundry para hóspedes de hotéis,
resorts e Airbnbs em Orlando. A imagem deve comunicar alívio, conveniência e tempo de
férias recuperado — sem parecer uma propaganda genérica ou uma imagem artificial de IA.

## Prompt principal para colar no Lovart

Create one premium, photorealistic website hero image for a guest laundry pickup and
delivery service in Orlando, Florida.

Scene: a candid, believable moment at the entrance or elegant lobby of an upscale
Orlando resort. A discreet laundry pickup professional is receiving one neutral,
unbranded white fabric laundry bag from a relaxed traveling couple or family. The guests
look relieved and ready to return to their vacation. In the soft background, show subtle
resort cues such as warm architectural lighting, tropical landscaping and a glimpse of a
pool — no theme-park characters, no copyrighted landmarks and no hotel brand names.

Composition: premium 4:5 portrait, 1600 × 2000 px, designed for the visual panel on the
right side of a split website hero. Keep the people and the handoff readable in the center
and lower two-thirds so the image remains clear on mobile crops. Preserve a clean safe area
near the UPPER-RIGHT edge for a small official brand overlay added later by the website.

Visual direction: premium hospitality editorial photography, natural Florida golden-hour
light mixed with refined deep-blue shadows, high contrast but realistic skin tones,
subtle depth of field, candid body language, authentic fabric texture, clean but not
sterile, sophisticated and trustworthy. The image should feel like a real commercial
photo shoot for a boutique concierge service, not stock photography.

Emotional message: “Enjoy Orlando. We handle the laundry.” Show freedom and relief
through the moment, but DO NOT add this sentence or any other text inside the image.

Strict rules:
- NO text, letters, numbers, prices, captions or watermarks.
- NO logos or invented branding on shirts, bags, vehicles or buildings.
- NO washing machines, laundromat interiors or piles of dirty clothes.
- NO plastic-looking faces, exaggerated smiles, extra fingers, distorted hands or
  impossible anatomy.
- NO Disney, Universal or other protected characters, castles, signs or trademarks.
- NO artificial neon glow, oversaturated HDR or obvious AI aesthetic.
- The laundry bag must be plain and unbranded.
- Leave the official A7 logo entirely out of the generated image; it will be composited
  later from the original transparent PNG.

Generate one final image focused on the front-desk or resort-entrance handoff. Do not create
a collage, split screen or alternate layout. Do not change the 4:5 aspect ratio.

## Seleção

Escolher apenas uma versão para a hero:

1. pessoas e mãos naturais;
2. sacola sem qualquer texto ou logo;
3. leitura imediata de “coleta de lavanderia para hóspede”;
4. espaço escuro e limpo à esquerda;
5. hotel/resort crível, sem marca de terceiros.
