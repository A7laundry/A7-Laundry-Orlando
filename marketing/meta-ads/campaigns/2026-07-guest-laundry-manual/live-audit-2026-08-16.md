## Initial decision

1. Keep the campaign active at US$20/day during the audit; do not scale solely from Meta's recommendation.
2. Preserve A4 as the volume control and both English ads as low-volume learning cells.
3. Do not reactivate LA7 in its current form; it spent US$22.18 without a conversation and had the weakest link CTR/CPC.
4. Prepare a native 9:16 regional guest challenger for Disney/Universal and test it without replacing A4.
5. Reconcile the 16 Meta conversations against qualified quotes and paid orders before any budget increase.
