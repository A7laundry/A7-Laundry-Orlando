FAQ

Paradise Palms Resort laundry, answered.

Do you offer laundry pickup at Paradise Palms Resort?
Yes. A7 Laundry offers free laundry pickup & delivery for Paradise Palms Resort and the surrounding Kissimmee area. Message us on WhatsApp at (407) 670-8839 with your unit address and pickup time — no app, no account.
Can Paradise Palms Resort hosts set up recurring turnovers?
Yes — hosts and property managers at Paradise Palms Resort can set up recurring turnover laundry with priority scheduling, at the same per-pound rate. No subscription and no contract.
How fast is delivery before check-in?
Next-day with Normal 24h service, or same-day with Express 6h depending on availability. Express requests are accepted until 6 PM and remain subject to unit availability; the return time is confirmed before pickup.
How much is laundry at Paradise Palms Resort?
Wash & fold is $3.25/lb (Normal 24h) or $3.95/lb (Express 6h), with free pickup & delivery and a $50 minimum order. Comforters are $33 Twin, $37 Full/Queen and $40 King.
Do you wash guest laundry too?
Absolutely. Beyond turnover linens and towels, we wash guest laundry too — personal wash & fold for families staying at Paradise Palms Resort, folded and delivered back to the unit.
How do I book?
Message us on WhatsApp at (407) 670-8839 with your Paradise Palms Resort unit or address and a preferred pickup time. No app or account required.
