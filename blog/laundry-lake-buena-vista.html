The part nobody puts in the brochure

What hotel laundry actually costs you.

Most hotels around Hotel Plaza Blvd have a guest laundry room. On paper it looks cheap. Here is how the evening really goes.

Three loads. One evening gone.

A family of four on a five-day trip generates about three loads — park clothes, swimwear, and a towel pile that grows after every pool afternoon.

  • Roughly $8 a load. Around $3.25–$3.50 to wash, the same again to dry, plus a dollar for detergent from the vending machine.
  • An app before a wash. Most machines moved to a QR-code payment app. You download it, create an account, and pay per cycle from your phone.
  • You have to stay close. Thirty minutes to wash, forty-five to dry, and a machine someone else may claim if you wander off.
  • Then you fold. At eleven at night, with the kids asleep and a park opening in nine hours.

We will be straight with you: in dollars alone, the laundry room is cheaper. What it costs is an evening of the trip you paid for — and that is the part you cannot buy back.

Questions guests actually ask

Lake Buena Vista laundry, answered.

Is there a laundry service that picks up from Lake Buena Vista hotels?
Yes. A7 Laundry collects from hotels along Hotel Plaza Boulevard and across Lake Buena Vista, including properties near Disney Springs. You leave the bag with the front desk and we handle the rest — pickup and delivery are free.
How much does laundry cost in Lake Buena Vista?
$3.25 per pound for standard service returned within 24 hours, or $3.95 per pound for express in about 8 hours when capacity allows. Minimum order is $50, which is roughly 15 pounds. Pickup and delivery are included at no extra cost.
Do I have to be in my room for pickup?
No. Most guests leave the bag with the front desk or bell desk on the way to the parks. We coordinate the handoff by WhatsApp so nothing interrupts your day, and we message you when the bag is collected and again when it is back.
How is this different from using the hotel laundry room?
The guest laundry room costs roughly $8 a load once you count the wash cycle, the dry cycle and detergent — and most machines now require a payment app on your phone. It also costs about three hours of your evening, plus folding. We are more expensive in dollars and cheaper in vacation time. You do nothing except hand over a bag.
Can you do laundry the same day?
Often, yes. Requests until 6 PM go into the express flow, which returns your laundry in about 8 hours and is subject to availability. After 6 PM we confirm case by case. Standard service is a 24-hour turnaround.
Do I need an app or an account?
No app, no account, no subscription. Everything happens over WhatsApp — you message us, we confirm the pickup, and you pay by card once we have weighed the order.
Do you wash everything together?
No. Orders are washed separately per customer, and we sort by colour and fabric. If something needs special handling — delicates, a suit, a comforter — tell us when you message and we will confirm the price and turnaround before pickup.
Do you serve vacation rentals in Lake Buena Vista too?
Yes. We pick up from villas, condos and vacation rentals across Lake Buena Vista and the surrounding area, and we work with hosts on recurring turnover schedules. Message us with the address and we will confirm coverage.
