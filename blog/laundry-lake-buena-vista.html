Straight answers

Before you leave the bag.

Do you pick up laundry from Lake Buena Vista hotels?
Yes. Send your hotel name or address and needed-by time on WhatsApp. A7 confirms coverage, the available pickup window and the handoff point before collection.
Can I leave my laundry with Bell Services or the front desk?
If your hotel permits an outside-vendor handoff, you can leave a labeled suitcase or securely closed bag with Bell Services or the front desk. If it does not, A7 coordinates another meeting point with you.
Do I need to wait at the hotel for pickup?
Not when the hotel approves the handoff. A7 confirms the exact arrangement by WhatsApp before you leave the bag.
Can the laundry return before checkout?
Share your checkout or needed-by time first. A7 confirms whether Standard or Express can fit that deadline before pickup; a return time is not guaranteed until confirmed.
How quickly can Express laundry return?
Express targets a return in up to 8 hours only when availability, capacity and the return window are confirmed. Standard service targets an approximate 24-hour return.
Can I send the laundry in a suitcase?
A suitcase or securely closed bag can work. Before pickup, A7 confirms how to identify it and where the hotel permits the handoff.
Can I request no dryer or special care?
Share every special-care instruction before pickup. A7 confirms whether the requested process is available and whether it changes the price or turnaround.
How much is hotel laundry pickup in Lake Buena Vista?
Standard starts at $3.25 per pound and Express starts at $3.95 per pound. The minimum order is $50, and pickup and delivery are included in the confirmed service area.
