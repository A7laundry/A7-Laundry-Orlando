Before you message

Clear answers for resort guests.

Can Orlando resort guests get clean clothes ready for tomorrow's plans?
Yes, within the confirmed A7 service area. Send the resort or address and tomorrow's exact needed-by time. A7 confirms coverage, pickup options and the return window before collection.
Can I leave laundry with Bell Services or the front desk?
Only when the property permits an outside-vendor handoff. A7 confirms the approved arrangement before pickup; otherwise, we coordinate another meeting point with you.
Do I need to wait at the hotel for pickup?
Not when the property approves the handoff. Send your plans first and A7 will confirm where and when the bag can be collected.
Can my laundry return before checkout, a flight or my next hotel?
Send the exact needed-by time before pickup. A7 confirms whether Standard or Express can fit the window; the deadline is not guaranteed until it is confirmed.
How quickly can Express laundry return?
Express targets a return in up to 8 hours only when availability, capacity and the return window are confirmed. Standard targets an approximate 24-hour return.
Can I send laundry in a suitcase or closed bag?
A suitcase or securely closed bag can work. A7 confirms how to identify it and the permitted handoff point before collection.
What should I send to request hotel pickup?
Send the hotel or address, optional room number, checkout or needed-by time, approximate bag or load size, and whether you prefer Standard or Express.
How much is Orlando hotel laundry pickup?
Standard starts at $3.25 per pound and Express starts at $3.95 per pound. The minimum order is $50. Pickup and delivery are included only in the confirmed service area.
