FAQ

Same-day drop-off, answered.

How does same-day drop-off laundry work in Orlando?
Message us on WhatsApp at (407) 670-8839 in the morning. Drop your laundry off or have us pick it up free from your door — nobody needs to be home. We wash and fold at commercial temperatures and deliver it back the same day with Express 6h service, subject to availability.
What's the cutoff for same-day return?
For the best chance of same-day return, get your laundry to us by 6 PM. Same-day Express turnaround is 6 hours and is subject to availability, so message early — especially if you have a flight or event the next day.
Do you also pick up, or is it drop-off only?
Both. You can drop off, but most customers just have us pick up — free pickup & delivery is included at no extra charge across Orlando, Kissimmee, Lake Nona, International Drive, Winter Park and Downtown. No app or account required.
How much is same-day Express laundry?
Express 6h same-day wash & fold is $3.95/lb, or $3.25/lb for Normal 24h service. Free pickup & delivery with a $50 minimum order. Comforter cleaning from $33. See full pricing.
Is same-day laundry return guaranteed?
Same-day Express is subject to availability — we don't lock an exact hour, but if you message us early in the day we'll confirm whether a same-day return is possible before we collect. Standard Normal service is next-day (24h).
How do I book same-day drop-off laundry in Orlando?
Message us on WhatsApp at (407) 670-8839 with your Orlando address and whether you want to drop off or have us pick up. Tell us your deadline and we'll confirm same-day availability. No app or account needed.
