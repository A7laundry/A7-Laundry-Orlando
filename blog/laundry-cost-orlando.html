The short answer

How much does laundry cost in Orlando?

In Orlando, full-service wash & fold laundry costs about $3.25 per pound for standard 24-hour turnaround, or $3.95 per pound for 6-hour Express — with free pickup & delivery and a $50 minimum order. Because it's priced by weight, you only pay for what you actually send. A laundromat costs less per load in coins but adds hours of your own time, while hotel valet laundry is usually billed per item and can climb fast for a full suitcase.

Why price by the pound

One number. Nothing hidden.

Most Orlando price questions come down to one frustration: services that won't show a number until you're already committed. A7 does the opposite — $3.25/lb Normal, $3.95/lb Express, published up front.

Per-pound pricing is the fairest way to charge for laundry. A light week of t-shirts and gym clothes weighs less than a heavy week of jeans and towels, and you pay accordingly. No per-item counting, no surprise line items, no membership to unlock a "real" rate.
