What builds up in a mattress

You spend a third of your life on it.

Night after night a mattress soaks up sweat, body oils, skin cells and moisture — and that's exactly what dust mites and allergens feed on. Vacuuming the surface never reaches it, and a mattress can't go through the wash.

A7 brings a professional deep clean to your bedroom: hot-water extraction pulls the buildup out from within, we treat stains, and we sanitize the surface — so the bed you sleep on is genuinely clean, not just made.

FAQ

Mattress cleaning, answered.

How does mattress cleaning work?
We use professional hot-water extraction — a deep clean that lifts sweat, body oils, dust and allergens out of the mattress, treats stains, and sanitizes the surface. It's a cleaning & sanitizing service done on-site: your mattress is not collected like laundry.
Do you come to my home?
Yes. Mattress cleaning is an in-home service — our technician comes to your address in Orlando, Kissimmee, Lake Nona, Windermere, Dr. Phillips or Davenport and cleans the mattress where it is. Nothing is picked up or transported.
How long does it take to dry?
Most mattresses are dry within a few hours after hot-water extraction, depending on airflow and humidity. We recommend giving it time to fully air out before making the bed.
Do you remove stains and dust mites?
Yes. Our deep clean targets stains, sweat and body oils, and helps control dust mites and allergens that build up in a mattress over time — followed by sanitizing of the surface.
Do you clean mattresses in vacation rentals?
Yes. Vacation-rental and Airbnb hosts use us to keep guest mattresses fresh, stain-free and sanitized between stays. We can coordinate on your turnover schedule across Orlando, Kissimmee and Davenport.
