Frequently Asked Questions

How does laundry pickup & delivery work in Orlando?

Message us your hotel or Airbnb location and preferred pickup window. We confirm the handoff, wash and fold, and return the order in 24 hours with Normal service or in 8 hours with Express when available.

How much does it cost?

Wash & fold is $3.25/lb for Normal service (24h) or $3.95/lb for Express (8h), with a $50 minimum order. Pickup and delivery are included.

Do you pick up from hotels and resorts?

Yes. We pick up from hotels, resorts, Airbnbs and vacation rentals across the Orlando area. Text us your hotel name and room or your Airbnb address and a pickup time.

Can I get same-day laundry in Orlando?

Express targets an 8-hour turnaround when the unit confirms capacity. Requests are accepted until 6 PM; later requests are evaluated case by case.

What areas do you serve?

Orlando and the surrounding tourist corridor — Kissimmee, Lake Buena Vista, the Disney and Universal areas, Reunion Resort, Champions Gate, Davenport, International Drive, plus Orlando neighborhoods like Lake Nona, Dr. Phillips and Winter Park. See all areas.

What clothes are included in per-pound wash and fold?

Everyday machine-washable clothes such as T-shirts, shorts, socks, underwear and pajamas can go in the per-pound order. Comforters, sneakers, dry-clean-only garments and items needing special care are not automatically included. Send a photo so we can confirm whether A7 can handle the item and provide a separate quote before pickup.

How do I book?

Message us on WhatsApp at (407) 670-8839 with your hotel, resort or Airbnb location, preferred pickup window and approximate bag size. We confirm availability before pickup.
