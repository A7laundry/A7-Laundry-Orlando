Orlando guest laundry questions

What is A7 Laundry Orlando?

A7 Laundry Orlando is an independent guest wash and fold pickup and delivery service for travelers staying at hotels, resorts and vacation rentals in the Orlando area. A7 confirms coverage, price, handoff and return timing before pickup.

Does A7 Laundry pick up from Orlando hotels and resorts?

Yes, after confirming the property and pickup options. Send the hotel or resort name, your needed-by time and approximate load. Bell Services, the front desk or another handoff point can be used only when the property permits it.

Can A7 pick up from a vacation rental?

Yes, when the address is inside the confirmed service area. A7 coordinates a door or agreed no-contact handoff and confirms the pickup and return windows before collecting the order.

How does Orlando laundry pickup and delivery work?

Message A7 with your hotel, resort or vacation-rental location, needed-by time and approximate bag size. A7 confirms coverage, handoff, service pace and price; collects the identified bag; washes and folds eligible clothing; and returns it in the confirmed window.

How much does guest wash and fold cost?

Standard service starts at $3.25 per pound with an approximate 24-hour return. Express starts at $3.95 per pound and targets a return in up to 8 hours only when availability and capacity are confirmed. The minimum order is $50, and pickup and delivery are included in the confirmed area.

Is Express laundry guaranteed?

No. Express is offered only when A7 confirms availability, capacity, the pickup window and your needed-by time before collection. If Express cannot fit the deadline, A7 can explain the available Standard option.

Can laundry be returned before checkout or a flight?

A7 can evaluate a checkout, flight or next-hotel deadline. Send the exact needed-by time before pickup. A return window is confirmed only when the service pace and operating capacity can support it.

How are laundry bags identified and handed off?

A7 coordinates the agreed handoff and identifies the order before processing. At hotels, Bell Services or the front desk may be used only when the property permits it; otherwise A7 arranges another pickup point with the guest.

How can international guests pay?

A7 confirms the final total after weighing. A secure USD payment link hosted by Stripe is available, and A7 can also confirm whether Zelle, Venmo, Cash App or cash fits the order. Card details should never be sent through WhatsApp or SMS.

How do I request laundry pickup in Orlando?

Use WhatsApp or SMS and send your hotel, resort or vacation-rental location, needed-by time, approximate bag size and Standard or Express preference. A7 confirms availability before pickup.
